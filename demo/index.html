Why bilingual reading helps

Immersive translation keeps the original English paragraph visible and places a Chinese rendering directly underneath. This lets readers check terminology without leaving the page.

Large language models can preserve proper nouns, code identifiers, and URLs when the prompt explicitly asks them not to rewrite those tokens.

A short list

  • Select a phrase to open the floating translator.
  • Use the right-click menu to translate the whole article.
  • Remove the bilingual overlay at any time.
The goal is not to hide English, but to make English and Chinese readable together.
